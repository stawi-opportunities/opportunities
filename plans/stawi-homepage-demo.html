Why Stawi

Built for Africa, not exported to it

Most platforms list jobs abroad and force a currency, a bank account, and a visa reality that doesn't fit. Stawi starts with what works here — mobile money, local pay, deadlines that matter.

  • You're scored as a candidate
    Employers see a ranked shortlist, not a wall of identical CVs.
  • Pay the way you pay
    Subscriptions bill in USD via card or mobile money — no regional lockout.
  • No ghost listings
    Opportunities are checked and time-boxed. Deadlines are visible up front.
  • Browse before you sign up
    A live sample of real roles is public — prove there's something for you first.
